PURPOSE
Improve the existing 1:1 Geocraft LIDAR world of Southampton so buildings,
street furniture, water edges and materials match the real place, using
ground photos, aerial imagery and authoritative surveys. No fictional
districts, themes or whimsy — accuracy only.

ACCURACY RULES
   (Detail + generator widths: site/GEOCRAFT.txt. Source: ~/Projects/geocraft.)
0. GEOCRAFT TRUST (user, 11 Oct 2026): road *routes* and building *plan
   shapes* from OSM+LIDAR are usually good; road/pavement *widths* are
   arbitrary fixed buffers (OSM width tags ignored). Do not treat stock
   kerb/road edges as survey — trim or widen to aerial/street evidence.
   Keep corridor + footprint; replace materials/openings/roofs from photos.
1. Match real footprint, height band and openings before decorating.
   Footprint is a guide: square for Minecraft (±1–2 OK) — see
   site/CRAFTSMANSHIP.txt. Never put walls on the pavement (I-009).
2. Materials should read as the real surface (brick, stone, render, roof
   type) within Minecraft’s palette — not Geocraft’s brick/carpet defaults.
   Roofs: sealed pitch (usually slab 1:2 here), not Geocraft lattice;
   glass needs solid flanks (CRAFTSMANSHIP.txt).
3. Prefer small, reversible kit builds with backup + cells archive.
4. Do not invent features with no evidence. If the exact house has no photo,
   nearby houses of the same terrace / period / typology may be used as
   *hints* for materials, openings rhythm, and eaves (user, 11 Oct 2026) —
   mark the build/notes as hint-based, prefer the closest match, and never
   override footprint or height fixed by LIDAR/aerial for that plot.
5. HEIGHT (user-ready rule, 11 Oct 2026): Geocraft/LIDAR Y is the massing
   authority (terrain and overall building volume). Photos settle storey
   count, eaves/ridge *relative to neighbours*, and openings — do not
   fight absolute real-world metres into Minecraft Y without a written
   exception in the place brief.
6. PRIVACY + WHOLE PROPERTIES (user, 11 Oct 2026): rebuild whole properties
   — house on all sides, roof, front garden, back garden layout. Private
   houses get storey FLOORS + one stair only: no rooms, partitions or
   furniture, and no research of interiors. Back gardens from the AERIAL
   only (fences, lawn/patio, sheds, trees); no garden furniture, no
   over-the-fence photos. Public-building interiors only with explicit
   interior references.
7. Reference logging: fetch once, judge once — see reference/LOGGING.txt
   (manifest.jsonl + sparse wm/mc tiles). Do not re-download or re-Read
   useless images.
8. DONE BAR per property (default): footprint within ~1–2 blocks of
   OSM/aerial after squaring; airtight shell + flanked windows; roof form
   from aerial with pitch/overhang per CRAFTSMANSHIP.txt (Y from LIDAR);
   all elevations with openings (street side from photos, sides/rear from
   aerial + same-terrace hints); floors + stair inside; front boundary +
   garden matching the photo; back garden fences/lawn/patio/sheds/trees
   matching the aerial; routes walkable; readback clean; osm_footprints
   check OK; evidence views logged.
   OUT of default done: chimney pots/TV aerials, garden furniture, planting
   detail, rooms/furniture. Raise the bar only in the place brief.
9. GOOD ENOUGH (user, 11 Oct 2026): big project — do not obsess a detail
   burning tokens. Hit the done-bar, log, move on.
10. RADIAL COVERAGE (user, 11 Oct 2026): rebuild ALL of an area — no gap
    holes. Work outward from the current focus (default: Portswood Rd ×
    Broadlands Rd; the focus point itself is on Broadlands Rd, the real
    junction is MC X7198 Z-5461). Coverage is per parcel (scripts/coverage.py,
    Q-038): sign parcels off after builds; COVERAGE.txt cells are generated.
    Focus may change; update COVERAGE FOCUS when it does.
11. REVISITS (user, 11 Oct 2026): improving already-processed ground is
    allowed (snags, better evidence) but is not the focus — keep expanding
    the unfinished front unless a queued ISSUE says otherwise.
12. FRONTAGE AUTHORITY (I-009, 11 Oct 2026): house fronts come from OSM
    footprints via scripts/osm_footprints.py frontage; plots via
    osm_footprints.py plots (refine fences from aerial). Never from "last
    solid block before the pavement". site_kit.apply() refuses building-like
    blocks outside footprints (>2 high street side, >4 in back gardens). Street names come from OSM
    addr:street — the focus label is not a street name.
13. EVIDENCE CHECK: readback/walk/patrol only prove blocks match the plan.
    Done needs a view from the reference photo's camera beside the photo, a
    top-down view beside the aerial, and 3 written matches/mismatches. A build that needs a geometry fix build is
    a survey error: revert and re-survey, do not patch.

WISDOM KEPT FROM THE TOWN KIT (without town fiction)
- One builder at a time (site/LOCK.json).
- Files are memory; one queue item per chat.
- Plan offline → preview PNG → dry-run guards → apply → readback → walk
  → contact-sheet views → records.
- Never edit a BUILT plan in place: write a new modification build that
  declares what it modifies.
- Modifications come from the cells archive, not from regenerating a plan
  that re-reads terrain as if it were virgin.
- Fill unloaded chunks silently fails — hop the player near the box.
- Posts (fence/wall/bars) must not float on bottom slabs / carpets.
- Review via contact sheets; open full-size views only for detail.
- Batch anything that needs the user into one ask; do not poll.
