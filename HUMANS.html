Southampton Refine: for humans

A Minecraft rebuild of a real place (the University of Southampton Highfield campus and the streets around it), done cell by cell by AI agents and reviewed by people. This page is for the people: where to look, what to check, and how to steer. The machine-facing entry point is AGENTS.md. Links are relative, so open this file from the project folder.

Reviewing a cell

  1. Find what to look at. Each cell has its own page under site/pages/ with the images already made for it. Each run-log block has Look at lines with Minecraft x,z coordinates. In the game, teleport with /tp @s x ~ z (or ask the agent to take you there) and fly up to look down.
  2. Compare with the reference. Agents work from OSM footprints, LIDAR (heights), aerial imagery and photos. To see the aerial for any patch with OSM outlines and a 10-block grid: 
    .venv/bin/python scripts/aerial_annot.py x0,z0,x1,z1 out.png 8 reference/aerial/highfield-campus/mosaic-z18-8x8-centre-130055-87834.png
    For the sharp Google screenshot you supplied (registered to the grid, about 1 block accurate near its tie points): 
    .venv/bin/python scripts/aerial_registered.py crop reference/aerial/highfield-user/googlemaps-music-law-annex-2026-10-15.registration.json x0,z0,x1,z1 out.png
    More registered screenshots in reference/aerial/highfield-user/ are picked up automatically by the ground layer.
  3. Judge by the bar: accurate to the real place and street-readable, then move on. “Good enough” is deliberate (rules/radial-good-enough.md). Chimney pots and garden furniture are not the point.
  4. Tell the agent what you found in plain words and coordinates, or add a dated line under the relevant issue in ISSUES.txt. Prefer reporting a symptom; fixes go into the process (I-057), not into one-off patches.
Aerial caveats. The campus mosaics are blurry or blank in places (I-042). Roof colours on many houses are hints, not read from imagery. A sharp, registered screenshot beats both.

Concepts in one place

TermMeaning
Cell tx,tzA 128 by 128 block square. Cell 47,-45 covers x 6016..6143, z -5760..-5633 (x = tx*128, z = tz*128). North is smaller z.
RingDistance from the origin cell 48,-44 (ring 0). Cells are finished ring by ring, north-centre of the ring first, then clockwise.
MarkerA black-and-yellow wool ring about 30 blocks up around the cell being worked on. It only shows where agents are working. It moves on when a cell is finished down to decor.
LayersPer cell, in order: ground (surface blocks), trees, decor (hedges, fences). Buildings and canopies are separate “feature” builds. Trees and decor record a mask so they can be undone exactly.
Donesite_handoff.py cell_gaps is empty: layers applied, trees current, no unrefined Geocraft buildings left (except recorded exceptions).
Locksite/LOCK.json. One builder at a time. If it is held, do not edit the world. A stale lock (no heartbeat for 90 minutes) can be taken over, never edited by hand.
STAGESsite/cells/<tx>_<tz>-STAGES.json: what was applied to a cell, when, with readback counts and notes. The run log and status commands read these.
GeocraftThe older, LIDAR-derived world the work replaces. Good for height and mass, not for detail (rules).
LumpA leftover unrefined Geocraft building. lump_scan.py finds them. Some OSM building=roof shapes are actually trees (see way 444, I-055).

Rules worth knowing before you touch anything

  • Accuracy to the real place only. No invented districts or whimsy.
  • One builder at a time. Check site_lock.py status first. Never delete LOCK.json.
  • Never place plain dirt; use coarse_dirt (plain dirt turns to grass).
  • Process, not patches. A recurring fault is fixed in the layer that causes it, with a test.
  • Hand-made cells 48,-44 and 48,-45 are good as they are. Agents may only re-run trees there after checking.
  • World data is not committed; backups/, big PNGs and caches are git-ignored. Nothing is committed unless you ask.
If the app or a run died mid-way: check site_recover.py status before anything else. A stale lock plus an in-flight stage means recover first; a fresh lock means wait.
